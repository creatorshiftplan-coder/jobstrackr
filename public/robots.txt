# AI crawlers that index the whole catalogue but send few clicks back.
# Deliberately NOT disallowed — staying in Meta AI / ChatGPT answers is worth
# real traffic. They are throttled instead.
#
# In the Aug-2026 Vercel log window meta-externalagent was 43% of ALL requests
# (114 of 263) and paced itself at ~15.6s between hits against the Crawl-delay
# of 10 below, i.e. it honours the directive. Raising its delay to 60 cuts its
# request volume ~4x while still letting it reach every page, just slower.
#

# (link-preview unfurling), which stays fully allowed above so Facebook and
# WhatsApp share cards keep working.
